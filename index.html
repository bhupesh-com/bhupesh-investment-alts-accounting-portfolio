Interactive Technical & Behavioral Interview Mastery Frameworks
Rigorous interview preparation modules for Senior Alternative Investment Accountants & Fund Analysts
1. Structured Technical Interview Framework
Focus: Metrics, ASC 820 fair value audit, waterfall mechanics, and forensic performance scrutiny.
Q1: How do Subscription Credit Lines distort reported IRR vs actual DPI?
Top-1% Answer: Subscription lines delay capital calls by borrowing on fund lines of credit. Because IRR is sensitive to time, delaying the initial cash outflow artificially inflates interim Net IRR by 300–800 bps while leaving actual cash MOIC and DPI unchanged. Institutional LPs must demand un-levered Net IRR disclosure under ILPA guidelines.
Q2: What forensic red flags distinguish Level 3 mark-to-model inflation?
Top-1% Answer: Look for: (1) Divergence between rising paper RVPI and stagnant cash DPI; (2) Favorable comp selection during public peer sell-offs; (3) Delayed write-downs on portfolio companies missing budget; (4) Gross-to-Net IRR gaps exceeding 350 bps without explanatory operational expense justification.
Q3: Compare European vs American waterfalls from an LP risk perspective.
Top-1% Answer: European (whole-fund) waterfalls require 100% of all contributed capital plus hurdle to be returned across the fund before GP receives carry, eliminating GP clawback risk. American (deal-by-deal) waterfalls award carry on early winning exits, exposing LPs to severe clawback risk if later deals incur losses.
